Michigan rate model: figures 1 to 10

What data center load is worth to DTE Electric's other customers, and how far it goes toward the bill for Michigan's new regional lines, in three worlds: no federal permitting bill (A), the bill with no state action (B), and the bill plus the Michigan statute (C). Every number is read from the workbook, which is built on DTE's cost studies in Case U-22046, the Saline and Van Buren orders, and MISO's published line costs. Figure 7 adds what the bill's gas price effects could do to the same household, Figure 8 the carbon at stake in keeping data centers on the grid, and Figures 6 and 9 the exit to on-site gas and what moves the result.
