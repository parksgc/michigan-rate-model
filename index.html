Can data centers pay for Michigan's new power lines?

A federal permitting bill, BAAJA, would let states charge data centers more than it costs to serve them and credit the difference to everyone else. Pick a world and move the dials to see what new data center load is worth to a DTE household, and how much of the bill for the region's new transmission lines it covers.

Which world
Beyond the two approved sites. DTE reports 5 to 6 GW more in its pipeline.
This happens under Michigan's rules today, with no new law. A data center on DTE's standard rate pays more than the extra cost of serving it. The Commission sets DTE's rates to cover its total costs, so at the next rate case that difference lowers everyone else's rates. How big it is is disputed: the record runs from $5.46 (MPSC Staff) to $27.51 (DTE's claim for Saline), and the buttons set those figures. It cannot go much past $27, because a data center pays about $61 per MWh and fuel alone costs DTE about $34.
