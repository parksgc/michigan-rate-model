Can data centers pay for Michigan's new power lines?

A federal permitting bill, BAAJA, would let states charge data centers more than it costs to serve them and credit the difference to everyone else. Pick a world and move the dials to see what new data center load is worth to a DTE household, and how much of the bill for the region's new transmission lines it covers.
